# OS-intent — pitch (EN + FR)

Tone: fast, confident, "spaceship cockpit": short sentences, one beat per cut. Every claim maps to a real feature
(features.md). No speed numbers, no user counts, no claims of AI "understanding" beyond what is shown.

Grounding (what we can say, and why):
- **"Your next decision, one key away"**: predicted options at fixed places; a bare modifier tap runs one (fn 1, ⌃ 2, ⌥ 3, ⌘ 4).
- **"It notices when something changes"**: opt-in local sensors (Mail, Telegram agents, chosen folders, Calendar, GitHub PRs) turn a new state into a ringed hint, gone when the state ends.
- **"It tells you why it matters"**: one relevance judge weighs every event against your own knowledge base (a local folder of markdown pages); the level-3 card shows the page that makes it matter.
- **"It carries your decisions forward"**: presses, dismissals and wishes become labels; day 30 is not day 1.
- **"Nothing leaves without your yes"**: every send (voice note, screenshot, reply, page press) waits for the outward gate's panel.
- **"Local-first, and honest about its health"**: raw activity stays on the Mac; local-only domains never reach a model; Claude only through your own subscription; each sensor can be switched off; `oi health` makes failures visible.

## English

**1 line**
> Your Mac learns what you do next, and puts it one key away.

**3 lines**
> OS-intent learns how you work, on your Mac, and puts your next move one key away.
> When something changes, a mail, an agent, a file, it notices, and tells you why it matters.
> Hold to go deeper, speak to hand off to an agent. Nothing leaves without your yes.

**30 s voice-over** (≈ 80 words)
> Day one, your Mac knows your apps. Day thirty, OS-intent knows your work.
> Four dots by your pointer. One tap. Done.
> Hold, and it tells you what's behind each move, and why it matters.
> You didn't touch a thing? A client wrote. It noticed.
> Talk to an agent, or type. Add your screen.
> Nothing leaves your Mac without your yes.
> OS-intent. Your next decision, one key away.

**90 s voice-over** (≈ 230 words)
> You know your next move before you make it. Your computer doesn't. Until now.
> On day one, OS-intent knows your apps. On day thirty, it knows your work: the same client mail, and the cross already says "Reply to Sam", "Friday", "Dana".
> Four tiny dots. Tap Option-Command: four next steps, at four fixed places. Your fingers learn them once. One tap, and it's done.
> Now hold. Rest on a move: a card tells you what it's about, and why it matters, straight from your own notes. Glide. Release. Three steps, one move.
> Hold the key a little longer: Claude drafts the reply. You still press Send.
> Hands off the mouse. A mail lands, an agent writes, a file appears. The dots come back with a ring: something changed. One key, and you're on it.
> Your agents are waiting? The right chat, one click away.
> Control-Option: pick an agent, speak, or just type. Add your screen if it helps.
> And on a pull request, the button you were about to look for is already there. Every outward action asks first: send, merge, reply. Nothing leaves without your yes.
> Local first. Your subscription, not an API meter. Every sensor is yours to switch off, and nothing fails in silence.
> OS-intent. Your next decision, one key away.

> Et sur une pull request, le bouton que vous alliez chercher est déjà là. Chaque action vers l'extérieur demande d'abord : envoyer, fusionner, répondre. Rien ne sort sans votre accord.
> Local d'abord. Votre abonnement, pas un compteur d'API. Chaque capteur se coupe d'un clic, et rien ne tombe en panne en silence.
> OS-intent. Votre prochaine décision, à une touche.
